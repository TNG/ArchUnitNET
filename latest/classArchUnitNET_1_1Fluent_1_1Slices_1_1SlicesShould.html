Detailed Description

Provides condition methods for slice rules. The two available conditions are:

  • BeFreeOfCycles – asserts that no cyclic dependencies exist between slices.
  • NotDependOnEachOther – asserts that no slice depends on any other slice. This is a stricter condition than cycle-freedom.
