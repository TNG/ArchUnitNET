Detailed Description

Assigns types to slices by matching their namespaces against a pattern.

A pattern is a namespace with these tokens in it:

(*)captures exactly one segment
(**)captures one or more segments
(A|B)captures one of the alternatives
*matches one segment without capturing it
..matches zero or more segments
[A|B]matches one of the alternatives

A pattern must contain at least one capture group, may not mix (*) and (**), and may use (**) at most once. Apart from these tokens, it may only contain the characters of namespace segments, so regex syntax such as "+" or "?" is rejected. Types whose namespace does not match are left out of the rule.

A slice is identified by what the capture groups captured, in group order (SliceIdentifier.Parts): types that captured the same values share a slice. "App.(*).(*)", "App.(*).Service.(*)" and "App.(*)..(*)" all put the types they match in "Orders" and "Http" into the slice [Orders, Http], while "App.(**)" over "App.Orders.Http" captures the single value [Orders.Http].

The slice is named after its parts, e.g. "Slice Orders - Http"; see SliceIdentifier.Description.
